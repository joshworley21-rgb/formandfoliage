# Bundled fonts

The native app bundles these Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1:

- Bricolage Grotesque — display family. Copyright Atelier Triay.
- IBM Plex Sans — body family. Copyright IBM.
- IBM Plex Mono — figures and labels. Copyright IBM.

License text: https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

The files live in `app/src/main/res/font/` and are referenced from
`ui/theme/Type.kt`.
